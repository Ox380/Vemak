# VEMAK — Landing Page Wireframe v0.1

Language: Lithuanian. Primary CTA: **"Prašyti pilotinio bandymo klasei"** (request a pilot for your class). Secondary: **"Išbandyk demo"**.

## Sections, top to bottom

1. **Hero**
   - Headline: *Finansinis raštingumas, kurį mokiniai nori žaisti.*
   - Subline: 6 savaičių žaidimas klasei. Mokiniai priima sprendimus, mokytojai mato rezultatus.
   - Buttons: Pilotas / Demo. Image: the city-map screen.

2. **Try it now (live demo, no login)**
   - One real weekly decision (the sneakers-vs-business screen), then a short result and a mentor line.
   - Ends with "Nori visos savaitės? Pradėk pilotą."

3. **How a week works** — Mon / Tue-Wed / Thu / Fri strip, with a screenshot for each.

4. **Three audience tabs**

   | Tab | Headline | Benefits |
   |---|---|---|
   | **Mokytojams** | Pamoka paruošta už jus | Weekly lesson plans, no setup beyond a class code, one-click CSV/PDF export, see who struggles on which topic |
   | **Mokiniams** | Išmok klysti be tikrų pinigų | Real decisions with consequences, race your class without copying (secret strategies), personal AI mentor, second chance after failing |
   | **Mokyklos vadovybei** | Aiškūs rezultatai ir saugūs duomenys | Measurable progress per class, no student emails, virtual money only, pilot with no obligation |

5. **What students learn** — budgeting, saving, investing and diversification, risk and reserves, running a small business. (Say "curriculum aligned" only after decision 18 is done.)

6. **Teacher dashboard preview** — participation, missions completed, knowledge change, skills by topic, before and after.

7. **Safety and privacy** — virtual money only, no student emails, the AI mentor never sees names, EU hosting (once confirmed), data deleted on request. Link to the full policy.

8. **How the pilot works** — 1. Request, 2. We set up your class, 3. Six weeks of play, 4. Results report.

9. **FAQ** — time per week, devices needed (any browser), cost, GDPR, curriculum fit.

10. **Pilot request form** — school, municipality, teacher name and email, grade, approximate class size, consent checkbox.

11. **Footer** — contact, privacy policy, terms.

## Do not put on the page until verified

- "Atitinka Lietuvos mokymo programą" (curriculum claim)
- Any percentage result such as "+18 % žinių pokytis" (that is mock data on your board; use only real pilot data)
- Any statement about GDPR compliance or hosting location

## Visual direction

Navy, teal and white with yellow, green and red accents, taken from your board. Rounded cards, isometric city illustrations. The demo uses the same components as the real game, so the landing page doubles as a test of the UI.

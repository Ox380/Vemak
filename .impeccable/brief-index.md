# Surface brief — app/public/index.html

Mode: Persuade. One page: the landing offer for teachers and individuals.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is a market board, not a product page. Every offer hangs as a hand-written price tag on string, and prices are crossed out and rewritten the way a real market rewrites them. It refuses the category default: a headline over subcopy, one button, and a row of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Kraft card ground with drawn fibre grain; black marker lettering with red corrected figures; string, pegs and brass eyelets as the only chrome; panels cut by drawn rules into unequal wedges; one dense price band that slides. Nothing is a rounded white card on a pale ground.

STORY: A teacher sees a price that moves, understands in one line that this is a six-week season where decisions have consequences, and plays a real week on the spot — the demo is the action, not a link.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A wooden rail runs across the top with a hanging sign carrying the product name in marker lettering. Below it, three hanging tags on string: the largest is the primary action (play a week), the other two carry the offer in one line each. Right of them, a city plan pinned with two pegs. Along the bottom, a price strip shows the six weeks as tags with one crossed out.

FORM: Market stall price tags on a board.
